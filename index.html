Executive Summary
What matters, what's next, and the three decisions that need to happen this week.
Strategic Diagnosis · June 2026
Strong brand, proven market position — infrastructure not yet ready to scale.
Authorized Dealer is a 3-location (soon 4) NJ cannabis retailer with a #1 position in Jersey City's 28-competitor market, a licensed delivery channel, and the most differentiated brand in its territory. The business doubled JC revenue year-over-year.

It is now entering paid media scale-up from a position of operational vulnerability: the primary Instagram account is disabled, attribution is unverified, email marketing hasn't started, and the CRM is underutilized. The opportunity is real and time-sensitive — but only if the foundation is fixed first.

Attribution is unverified. The menu ordering flow may not be passing conversion events to Meta or GA4 — confirm whether pixel tracking fires through checkout before launching paid campaigns. Until verified with a test purchase, CPA data is an estimate, not a measurement.

No master vs. sub-brand hierarchy defined. With a 4th store opening, this becomes harder the longer it's deferred.

Meta Non-Negotiable Compliance Rules

No transactional language. All CTAs must be lifestyle-framed: "Explore," "Find a location," "Learn more." No "buy now."
No product close-ups. Show environment, lifestyle, and people — not flower, pre-rolls, vapes, or concentrates in isolation.
No pricing in creative. "$20 eighths" or any specific price point in ad creative is a compliance flag.
Multiple store accounts is standard practice. Gotham and comparable operators use separate accounts per location. This is risk management, not a gray area.
